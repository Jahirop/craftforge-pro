# Privacy Policy

**Craftforge.studio**

We build brands with intent, and we treat your data the same way. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit **craftforge.studio** (the "Site"), how we use it, and the control you have over it. No fine print games. Just a clear account of how things work.

---

## Who this applies to

This policy covers information collected on **craftforge.studio** only. It does not cover information collected through other channels, or through any third party platforms, tools, or partner sites we may link to, since those run under their own policies. Where a separate service of ours collects a different kind of data, we will say so at the point it is collected.

## Information you give us

When you reach out, whether by email, a project enquiry, or a contact form, you may choose to share personal details with us. That can include your **name, email address, phone number, company, or business address**.

We use what you share for one reason first: to answer you and move your project forward. We may also use it to follow up on related topics we genuinely think will be useful to you. You can opt out of that anytime (see *Your choices* below).

## Information we collect automatically

To keep the Site fast, relevant, and worth your time, we collect some technical information automatically when you visit. This typically includes your **IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, the pages and actions you take on the Site, and the date and time of your visit.**

This data is aggregated. It tells us how people use the Site so we can improve it, not who you are personally. We collect it through the methods below.

### Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit certain sites. They let the Site remember preferences, support security, and gather statistics, such as which pages get visited, what gets downloaded, and how visitors move through the Site.

You are in control. You can refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings. Just know that switching them off may limit some features of the Site. Your browser's "Help" section explains how to manage, block, or be notified about cookies.

### Pixels and web beacons

A web beacon (or pixel) is a tiny graphic embedded in a page or email. We use these, including the **Meta (Facebook) Pixel** and **Pinterest tag**, to understand traffic patterns and measure whether content was viewed. The information they collect is aggregated clickstream data and is not tied to you as an individual unless you have contacted us directly by email. By using this Site, you agree to our use of cookies and pixels as described here.

### Analytics

We use third party analytics, including **Google Analytics**, which rely on JavaScript and cookies to help us understand how the Site is used (for example, your IP address, the URLs visited, and the date and time of each view). These services compile activity reports for us and help us improve. You can refuse cookies via your browser as described above. Google's privacy practices for Analytics are explained at **policies.google.com/privacy**, and you can opt out using the Google Analytics opt out browser add on at **tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout**.
